## A Rozier, con copia a Terracol

Asunto: CST conjecture by number of local maxima (your Remark 5.5): a draft with Lean proofs

> Dear Dr. Rozier, dear Mr. Terracol,
>
> My name is Omar Said Duran. I am an independent researcher in Calama, Chile, without academic training in number theory. In Remark 5.5 of "Paradoxical behavior in Collatz sequences" you mention the idea, suggested by Simons, of studying the CST conjecture for sequences with a fixed number of local maxima. I have tried to carry this out, and I would be very grateful for an expert opinion.
>
> The attached draft transfers the Simons-de Weger method from cycles to the first crossing (congruences along the runs, the Laurent-Mignotte-Nesterenko bound and finite certificates). It shows that T^tau(n)(n) < n whenever tau(n) is finite and the parity vector up to tau(n) has at most 24 runs; with your Corollary 5.4 as an additional hypothesis, the same holds up to 77 runs. Since the draft, a weighting of the runs (an idea taken from Hercher's paper on m-cycles) raised this to 81 runs with your Corollary 5.4, and to 42 runs using instead only a computer verification up to 3*10^11. All steps are formalized in Lean 4; the LMN bound and the numerical verification enter as explicit hypotheses, and only the standard axioms are used.
>
> I must be transparent: the proofs, the code and the text were produced with extensive help from AI models (Claude and ChatGPT) under my direction, and this message was also written with that help, since my language is Spanish. That is exactly why I am looking for human eyes. If you have time, I would like to know:
> 1. whether this treatment by number of local maxima is already known or being done elsewhere;
> 2. whether you see a gap in the reduction to the first crossing (Sections 2 to 4 of the draft).
>
> This work has not been published anywhere. The Lean sources and the certificates are available on request. Thank you very much for your time; I fully understand if you cannot look at it.

> Dear Dr. Hercher,
>
> My name is Omar Said Duran. I am an independent researcher in Calama, Chile, without academic training in number theory. I have tried to transfer the method of Simons-de Weger and of your paper "There are no Collatz m-cycles with m <= 91" from cycles to the first crossing of the coefficient, in order to study Terras's coefficient stopping time conjecture by number of local maxima, as Rozier and Terracol suggest in Remark 5.5 of their paper (Discrete Math. 349, 2026). I would be very grateful for an expert opinion.
>
> The attached draft shows that T^tau(n)(n) < n whenever tau(n) is finite and the parity vector up to tau(n) has at most 24 runs, and up to 77 runs if the verification of Rozier and Terracol up to 2.8*10^19 is added as a hypothesis. Since the draft, the weighting of the runs from your paper raised this to 81 runs (with that verification), and to 42 runs using only our own computer verification up to 3*10^11. The fusion lemma of your paper did not seem to transfer to this setting. All steps are formalized in Lean 4; the Laurent-Mignotte-Nesterenko bound and the numerical verifications enter as explicit hypotheses, and only the standard axioms are used.
