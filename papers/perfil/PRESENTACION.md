## 3. Presentación larga (carta de presentación, respuesta a «tell us more»)

> Omar Javier Said Duran is an independent researcher from Chile, born in Copiapó and raised in Ovalle, in the Coquimbo Region. He does not hold an academic position.
>
> From the age of fifteen he attended the weekly classes that Sergio Larraín (1931–2012) gave in Ovalle. Larraín, one of the great photographers of the twentieth century and a member of Magnum from 1961, abandoned photography in 1969 for a life of contemplative practice in the mountains of northern Chile. In his last decades he wrote small books that he gave away for free, with a single declared purpose: to help humanity avoid its own self-destruction. Said Duran considers Larraín his teacher and has kept that purpose as his own.
>
> His first book, *La supremacía de la conciencia* (2022, now published in ten languages), is a memoir of that apprenticeship and an essay on consciousness. His second, *La holgura* (2026), was written by an AI under his direction and is declared as such; it is a natural history of how each new layer of civilisation consumes the safety margin of the layer below, and of why the same shock that a society once absorbed can later break it.
>
> Mathematics came later, as the one field where being right can be checked by a machine. Larraín used to say that "the universe eliminates contradictions"; a proof assistant does exactly that. Working with large language models as programming and proof assistants, Said Duran has produced a complete Lean 4 / Mathlib formalization proving a 1993 conjecture of Gao on the coalescence of consecutive Collatz trajectories (doi:10.5281/zenodo.23003500). He asks to be judged by whether the proofs are correct; every step is open to checking.

## Lo que no se dice, y por qué

- **Larraín no avala la matemática.** Murió en 2012; fue maestro, no
  aval del trabajo. Decir *endorsed by* sería falso y cualquier revisor lo
  comprobaría en un minuto. «Studied under» y «considers him his teacher»
  son ciertos y bastan.
- **La holgura no es «escrita por» Omar**: el propio libro declara un
  90 % de máquina. Se dice «written by an AI under his direction».
- **La IA en la matemática** se nombra también en la bio corta: coincide
