%N Number of new Collatz merging classes at level n with exactly 8 mixed-parity steps.
%C Let T be the Collatz map (A014682): T(x) = x/2 if x is even, T(x) = (3*x+1)/2 if x is odd. A new merging class at level n is a residue class modulo 2^n for which the pair (r,r+1) merges at step n with equal odd count and is not inherited from a merging class at level n-1.
%C This sequence counts the new classes whose two trajectories have opposite parity at exactly 8 of the n steps. The first nonzero term is for n = 9.
%C The companion sequences for 10 and 12 mixed-parity steps are A399820 and A399821. The lower strata have simple forms: the strata with 2 and 4 mixed-parity steps have one class at each level, and the stratum with 6 mixed-parity steps is 3 + (n-7)*(n-8)/2 for n >= 10.
%H Omar Said, <a href="https://github.com/theswordofthemorning/zootheorem">Statements, proofs, sequence data and verification scripts</a>
