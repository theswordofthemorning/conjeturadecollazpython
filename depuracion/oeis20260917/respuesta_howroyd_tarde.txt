Precisely, set x_i = T^i(r), y_i = T^i(r+1), with T = A014682. We count 0 <= r < 2^n when the paths first meet at i=n, their input lists at i=0,...,n-1 have equal numbers of odd terms, and their inputs have opposite parity at exactly eight positions. The other two entries replace eight by ten and twelve. The examples number the corresponding iterations 1,...,n.
