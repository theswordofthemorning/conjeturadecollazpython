The one-line criticality statement (see papers/02): writing each
shared step under the fresh-bit law as c -> m*c, the
multiplier m is a fair coin on {1/2, q/2}, so E[m] = (1+q)/4.

case uniquely has mean shared-step multiplier one. This is not a density theorem.
